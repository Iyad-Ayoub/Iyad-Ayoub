# Dr. Iyad Abuhadrous

**Senior R&D Engineer | Robotics, Localization & Multi-Sensor Fusion | Autonomous Systems**

Robotics and autonomous-systems researcher with 20+ years of experience in localization, sensor fusion, perception, mapping, control, and intelligent vehicles.

Currently a **Senior R&D Engineer with the INRIA–Valeo ASTRA Joint Research Team**, working on multimodal perception, autonomous-driving data pipelines, BEV-based methods, HD mapping, and simulation-based validation.

My background includes real-time vehicle localization using **GNSS/DGPS, IMU, odometry and Kalman-filter-based sensor fusion**, LiDAR perception, 3D mapping, and experimental autonomous-vehicle systems.

Current R&D also covers **CARLA, ROS2, Python/C++, sensor calibration, synchronization, BEV perception, and autonomous-driving perception and decision systems**.

> Some current R&D work is developed in private collaborative repositories and is not publicly available.

**Open to senior R&D and research opportunities in robotics, navigation, autonomous systems, and applied AI.**
